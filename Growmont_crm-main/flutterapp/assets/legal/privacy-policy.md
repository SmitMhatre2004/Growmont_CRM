# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Growmont CRM ("the App") is private business software. It is licensed to a
single organisation ("the Company") and issued directly to that organisation's
staff. It is not offered to the general public and is not distributed through
any public app store.

This policy explains what the App records, where that information is held, and
who can reach it.

---

## Who is responsible for your information

The Company decides what is recorded in the App, who may see it, and how long
it is kept. The Company owns all data entered into the App.

Growmont supplies and maintains the software. Growmont handles information only
as needed to run and support the App on the Company's behalf, and does not use
it for any purpose of its own.

If you are a member of staff and want to know why something about you is
recorded, ask your administrator. They control the account and the records.

---

## What the App records

**Your account.** The work email address used to sign in, your display name,
your role (administrator or employee), and the date the account was created.
Accounts are issued by your administrator using your work email address.

**Your work in the App.** The business records you create or edit, including:

- Client and contact records, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses and
  addresses entered by staff
- Sales records, including products, amounts and dates
- Interaction and follow-up notes
- Reminders and personal notes
- Files and images you attach, and spreadsheets you import or export

**Technical information needed to run the App.** The app version, the operating
system it is running on, whether the device is currently online, and error
details when something fails. This is used to keep the App working and to
diagnose faults.

The App does not use advertising, does not track you across other apps or
websites, and does not build any profile of you.

---

## What the App does not record

- It does not collect your location.
- It does not read your contacts, photo library, messages or call history. You
  choose individual files or images to attach, and only those are used.
- It does not record your keystrokes, your screen, or your activity outside the
  App.
- It does not collect payment or banking details from you.

---

## Where your information is held

**On Google Firebase.** The App uses Firebase for sign-in, for the main
database, for file storage and for server-side processing. Firebase is operated
by Google and is the App's primary store.

**On your own device.** The App keeps a local copy of your records so it keeps
working without a connection. On a desktop computer this sits in your personal
application-data folder; on a phone or tablet it sits in the App's private
storage area. It syncs back to Firebase when you are next online.

Because a local copy exists, anyone who can unlock your device and open your
account may be able to see recent records. Keep your device locked and do not
share your sign-in details.

**Sign-in tokens** are held in the operating system's secure credential store
(Keychain on Apple devices, Keystore on Android, the Credential Locker on
Windows), not in ordinary files.

---

## Who your information is shared with

Access inside the App follows your role. Administrators can see staff accounts
and company-wide records. Employees see their own work and the records assigned
to them.

Outside the App, information is disclosed only:

- To Google, as the operator of Firebase, in order to store and serve the data
- To Growmont staff supporting the App, at the Company's request and only as far
  as needed to resolve the issue
- Where the Company or Growmont is required to do so by law

Information is never sold, rented, or handed to advertisers or data brokers.

---

## Updates on desktop

The Windows desktop build checks for a new version by contacting GitHub, where
releases are published. This check sends only what is needed to request the
release information. It does not send your records, your account details or
anything you have entered in the App.

---

## Keeping information secure

- Traffic between the App and Firebase is encrypted in transit.
- Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication; the App never sees or stores
  your password itself.
- Only addresses on the Company's approved email domain can sign in.
- Server-side rules restrict what each account may read and write, so access
  limits are enforced by the server and not only by the App.
- Sign-in tokens are held in the operating system's secure credential store.

No system is completely secure. If you believe an account has been misused, tell
your administrator straight away so it can be suspended.

---

## How long information is kept

Business records stay in the App until the Company deletes them, because they
form part of the Company's own records. There is no automatic expiry.

When someone leaves, the Company should disable their account. Disabling removes
access but keeps the work they entered, since that belongs to the Company.

The local copy on a device is removed when you sign out or uninstall the App.

---

## Your choices

- **Correct something.** Records you can edit in the App can be corrected there.
  For anything else, ask your administrator.
- **See what is held about you.** Ask your administrator.
- **Have your account or records removed.** Ask your administrator. Whether
  business records can be removed is the Company's decision, as they are the
  Company's records.
- **Attachments.** Attaching a file or image is always your choice. The App
  asks each time and works without it.

Because accounts are issued and controlled by the Company, Growmont cannot act
on these requests directly. Requests go to the Company.

---

## Children

The App is workplace software for the Company's staff. It is not designed for or
directed at children, and accounts are not issued to them.

---

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with the next release of the
App and the date at the top changes. Continuing to use the App after an update
means the revised policy applies.
