Bottom bar, five at most

Green 800, 62h. Dashboard, Sales, Chats, Clients, More. Profile moves to the app bar.

Tables become cards

Name and one line of context left; one number and one status right. Everything else is in detail.

Dialogs become sheets

Forms rise as bottom sheets, radius 12 top with a drag handle. Short confirms stay dialogs, inset 12.

Thumb-sized targets

44pt minimum. The main action is full-width 48h at the bottom, within reach.

Type steps down once

Page title 32 → 24, metric 28 → 24, heading 22 → 20. Body and labels stay the same.

One column

Form fields stack. KPI tiles go two-up. Nothing scrolls sideways.

Landscape is still a phone

On Android and iOS, a shortest side under 500 keeps the phone layout even when the width passes 768.

Respect safe areas

The bottom bar and sheets sit above the home indicator; nothing hides under the notch or status bar.

Sidebar on wide screens

Green 800 → 900 gradient, 216 wide, collapses to 80 icons-only. Same destinations as the bottom bar.

Press, don't hover

Touch has no hover: every state a mouse user sees on hover must also exist as a pressed or selected state.
